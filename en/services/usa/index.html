Who it may suit

Permanent residence without an employer or investment capital

EB-1A, O-1 and EB-2 NIW address different situations and standards for professionals with achievements in IT, science, the arts, sport, business and other fields. O-1 is temporary; EB-1A and EB-2 NIW are immigrant classifications.

🚀 An immigrant route

EB-1A and EB-2 NIW can lead to permanent residence; timing depends on the case and visa availability.

🧭 Your own application

Eligible EB-1A and EB-2 NIW applicants may self-petition without an employer.

🌍 Different professional fields

May suit eligible professionals across a range of fields.

💡 No investment-capital requirement

These routes differ from investor programmes.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Are you a law firm?

No. We prepare evidence and case materials and work alongside your immigration attorney. We do not provide legal services.

How long does preparation take?

It depends on your starting record and the evidence that needs strengthening. We agree a plan and estimated preparation schedule after reviewing the case.

Can I self-petition?

EB-1A allows eligible applicants to self-petition without an employer. We help organise the materials you or your attorney may include in the petition.

What does a case review provide?

We discuss your achievements, identify strengths and gaps against relevant criteria, and suggest an evidence-preparation plan.
